# FiberOps Blockchain UI Theme

This portal redesign keeps FiberOps as an infrastructure/admin product rather than a trading or NFT marketplace UI.

## Visual direction

- Dark CKB/Fiber operator-console foundation
- Neon mint/cyan accents used for network health and telemetry
- Monospace labels for protocol/runtime data
- Subtle blockchain grid background
- Glass/low-contrast panels with chain-state borders
- Fiber network topology visualization with animated packet paths
- Channel health ring and operational KPI cards
- Consistent READY/WARNING/CRITICAL badges across operations pages

## Updated surfaces

- Global application shell, sidebar, header and search
- FiberOps brand mark and CKB control-plane identity
- Page hero system
- Operations Overview
- Payment Readiness styling
- Channel Health tables
- Reconciliation status panels
- Incident Center cards
- Inputs, buttons, code/RPC output and status badges

## Design intent

The UI is deliberately styled as Web3 infrastructure, not a speculative crypto/trading dashboard. The product thesis remains operational reliability for Nervos Fiber.
